## 🎯 Features
Apex Harvest provides several features to enhance your farming practices:
- **AI-Powered Predictions:** Get accurate crop yield forecasts based on historical data.
- **Personalized Optimization Tips:** Receive tailored guidance for maximizing your harvests.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Enjoy simple navigation and clear visuals, making it accessible for all users.

## 🌱 System Requirements
To ensure that Apex Harvest runs smoothly, ensure your system meets the following requirements:
- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or later, macOS 10.12 or later, or a modern Linux distribution.
- **RAM:** At least 4 GB.
- **Storage:** Minimum of 200 MB of free disk space.
- **Network:** Internet connection is required for full functionality.
